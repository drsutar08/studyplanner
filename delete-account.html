Delete your Studyly account

Last updated: 10 November 2026

You can delete your Studyly account and everything in it at any time. Deleting it is permanent.

In the app

  1. Open Studyly and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Privacy & data.
  3. Tap Delete my account. You get a few seconds to undo it before it goes through.

If you can't open the app

Email drsutar08@gmail.com from the email address your account uses, with the subject "Delete my Studyly account". We'll delete the account and reply to confirm. A parent or guardian can ask for a child's account to be deleted in the same way.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, name, school and profile photo.
  • Your study data: tasks, exams, classes, attendance, breaks, focus sessions, grades, goals, flashcards, achievements and settings.
  • Photos you attached to tasks.
  • Study groups you own, with their boards, and your posts and replies.
  • Error reports linked to your account.
  • Your copies in the nightly backups, which are removed along with the account.

What is kept

  • A Premium purchase is handled by Google Play or the App Store. Deleting your account doesn't cancel it: cancel it in Google Play or the App Store first, so you aren't charged again.
  • Your own saved copies of your data, if you exported them, stay with you.

Before deleting, you can save a copy of your data in Settings → Privacy & data → Save a copy of your data.
