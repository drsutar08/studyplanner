Study Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Study Planner helps students plan and track their studies: tasks, exams, classes, focus sessions and progress. This policy explains what the app keeps, why, who can see it, how long it's kept, and how to remove it. The same points are in the app under Settings → Privacy & data.

Each row in the database is locked to its account by database rules. No other user can read your data, and other accounts can't change it.

What's shared, and only when you choose

  • Study groups. When you share your timetable with a group, the group's members see your classes, exam dates and breaks. Posts and replies on a group's board show your name to the group.
  • Studying now. While your focus timer runs, the people in your study groups see your name, the subject and when the session ends. They never see the task. This isn't stored: it disappears when the timer stops or the app closes. You can turn it off in Settings → Study groups.
  • Progress reports go only to the person you send them to, by the app you choose to share with.
  • The AI helper, only when you use it. See below.

The AI helper

The AI helper uses Claude, an AI made by Anthropic. Only when you ask it, Study Planner's server sends Anthropic:

  • the notes you paste, for flashcards or a quiz;
  • or, for "What should I study now?", a short summary: task titles, due dates, exam dates, grades, attendance and focus times.

Your notes in tasks, your photos and your account details are never sent. The answer isn't stored on Study Planner's server, which keeps only how many times you asked that day. Anthropic handles what it receives under its own privacy policy.

Ads

  • Who sees them: every account that doesn't have Premium, in the Android and iPhone apps. Premium accounts see no ads, and the web app has none.
  • Students under 18: accounts that said at sign-up they're under 18, and older accounts with no age recorded, are marked to Google as under the age of consent. Google then shows them only non-personalised ads rated for general audiences (G). Adults get non-personalised ads rated up to PG.
  • Where: a banner under the quote on the Dashboard and one at the bottom of Progress, and now and then a full-screen ad at a natural break (when a break starts after a focus session, or when a finished flashcard review closes), at most once every 10 minutes. Never during a focus session, a review or a quiz.
  • Never personalised. The app asks Google AdMob for non-personalised ads only, for everyone. Google still receives what any ad needs: the device type, the device's advertising ID, an approximate location from the IP address, and how the ad was seen, to show it, limit how often, and prevent fraud. Your study data, notes and account details are never sent.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example in the EU and UK), Google's consent form asks first.
  • Google's use of that data is described in its privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps.

What the app never does

  • No personalised ads for anyone, and only general-audience (G) ads for anyone under 18.
  • No analytics or tracking tools.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, and no sharing with advertisers or data brokers.

Reminders and notifications are scheduled on your device. They aren't sent from a server.

How long it's kept

  • Your data: until you delete it or delete your account.
  • Nightly backups: each copy is kept for 14 days, so the service can recover from a mistake or a failure.
  • Error reports: deleted after 30 days.
  • AI usage counts: deleted after 30 days.

Your choices and rights

Everything is in the app:

  • See and change your data: it's all in the app, and your name, school and photo are in your profile.
  • Get a copy: Settings → Privacy & data → Save a copy of your data, or Settings → Data → Export. This saves one file with everything, which can also be restored.
  • Stop sharing: leave a study group, stop sharing a group you made, or turn off Show my focus sessions to my groups.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Privacy & data → Delete my account. This removes your account and everything in it straight away: your study data, photos, groups you own (with their boards), your replies, error reports and your copies in the backups. There are a few seconds to undo it in the app first.

Depending on where you live, you may have more rights over your data, such as asking for a copy or for it to be deleted. The options above cover those. You can also contact us.

Children

Study Planner is a study tool for students. At sign-up everyone chooses their age group. Anyone under 18 must confirm that a parent or guardian knows about it and agrees, and everyone agrees to the terms of use and this policy. The account keeps that choice, with the date and the version of the terms agreed to. A parent or guardian can ask for a child's account and data to be deleted using the contact below.

Security

  • In transit: data travels encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Access: each account can reach only its own data, enforced by the database.
  • Passwords: handled by Supabase's sign-in service and never stored by the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes, and the app's Privacy & data page is updated to match.
