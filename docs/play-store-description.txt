Studyly is the study planner that keeps your classes, homework, exams and revision in one place, so you always know what to do next.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
• Tasks with due dates, times, time estimates, priorities and subtasks
• Exams with countdowns, plus an exam prep plan that spreads revision across the days you have left
• Class timetable, breaks and holidays, all in one calendar
• Quick add: type "Physics worksheet tomorrow 30 min" or say it out loud
• Reminders that arrive when they help, and a widget for what's next

STAY FOCUSED
• Focus timer with breaks, streaks and badges
• See which friends in your study groups are studying now, and join them

REMEMBER MORE
• Flashcards that come back just before you'd forget them
• Quick review sessions with your progress at a glance

KNOW WHERE YOU STAND
• Grades, attendance and a what-if calculator for the marks you need
• Weekly review and progress reports you can share as a PDF with a parent or teacher

STUDY TOGETHER
• Study groups with a shared timetable, a board for notes and shared tasks
• You choose what to share, and you can stop at any time

AI STUDY HELPER (PREMIUM)
• "What should I study now?": a short plan from your tasks, exams and focus time
• Turn your notes into flashcards
• Practice quizzes made from your notes
• AI can make mistakes: check answers before you rely on them

MADE TO BE COMFORTABLE
• Light and dark themes, larger text and an easier-to-read font
• Your data syncs to all your devices and works offline
• Save a copy of everything, or delete your account, from Settings

PRIVACY
Your study data is locked to your account, and you decide what to share. No tracking tools, and your data is never sold. Students under 18 sign up with a parent or guardian's agreement.

FREE AND PREMIUM
Studyly is free to use for planning, focus, grades and flashcards, and shows ads. Premium adds the AI study helper, removes ads and gives more room for flashcards, photos and study groups. It's a subscription, billed monthly or yearly through Google Play, and you can cancel any time in Google Play.
