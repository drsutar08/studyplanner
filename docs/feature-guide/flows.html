4. Live study groups

Share a timetable with classmates. They get your classes, exams and breaks, and see changes as you make them. Private things (attendance, scores, notes, tasks) are never shared.

Owner: “Create live link”Choose what to share · up to 10 groups
→
Send the link or QR codeCopy, share, or scan

→
